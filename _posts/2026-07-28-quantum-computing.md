# Quantum computing

During my internship at Worldline, my manager was very concerned about the future arrival of quantum computing. Worldline is a company handling billions of payment transactions, offering services for card companies and banks. His concern was raised due to quantum computing’s capacity to break down some algorithms that ensure security between transactions (cryptography). His role was to ensure that, once quantum computing becomes commercialized, Worldline would do what is necessary to convert all previous algorithms into modern and secure ones. This topic increased my curiosity and I must write what I understand to share this incredible subject.
